Privacy Policy

Shinng respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the app stores, collects, uses, and shares when you use Shinng.

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Information We Store Locally

Shinng stores your expense-tracking data on your device. This local data is used only to provide the app’s core expense-tracking features.

  • Transactions, amounts, notes, dates, and categories
  • Budgets and budget periods
  • Currency, theme, reminder, and app preferences
  • Pending quick-entry records
  • Backup connection status and related metadata

Information Collected or Sent Off Device

Some Shinng features use third-party services. We do not intentionally send your raw financial records to analytics or crash reporting services.

Firebase Analytics

Used to understand app usage, screen views, and basic events such as login/logout.

Firebase Crashlytics

Used to collect crash reports, stack traces, diagnostic logs, app version, and device/runtime information to fix bugs.

Firebase Cloud Messaging

Used to send app notifications and reminder-related messages.

Google Sign-In

Used only if you choose to connect a Google account for cloud backup.

Supabase

Used for authentication and optional cloud backup storage.

Google Fonts

Used to display app fonts and may connect to Google services depending on platform/runtime behavior.

Cloud Backup

Cloud backup is optional and requires connecting a Google account. If enabled, Shinng may upload a backup file to Supabase containing your categories, transactions, notes, dates, user settings, currency, reminder settings, backup metadata, and Google account details used for backup identification, such as email, account ID, and profile photo URL.

After connecting a Google account, automatic backup may be enabled unless you turn it off in the app.

Permissions

Shinng may request or use the following permissions:

  • Internet/network access for Firebase, Supabase, Google Sign-In, fonts, and cloud backup
  • Notification permission for reminders and app notifications
  • Vibration for notification or interaction feedback
  • Boot-related permission to support notification/reminder behavior
  • Foreground service/data sync permissions for background-related app behavior

Shinng does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, or precise location.

How We Use Information

  • Provide expense tracking, budgeting, reminders, and app settings
  • Authenticate optional Google account backup
  • Upload, restore, and manage optional cloud backups
  • Send notifications and reminders
  • Monitor app stability and fix crashes
  • Improve app reliability and understand feature usage

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data may be processed by service providers needed to operate the app, including Google/Firebase and Supabase. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Cloud backup data remains in Supabase while backup is connected or until deletion is requested. Disconnecting a Google account from the app may stop future backup use but may not automatically delete existing cloud backup files.

To request deletion of cloud backup or account-related data, contact us at Bawender.y@gmail.com.

Security

We use standard service providers and secure network connections where supported. Local data is stored on your device, and cloud backup data is handled through Supabase. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we use reasonable measures to protect user data.

Children’s Privacy

Shinng is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
