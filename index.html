Fast Expense Logging

Add a transaction before the moment passes.

Shinng is built for quick manual expense entry. Start typing a category and fuzzy search brings the right match forward, so you do not waste time digging through selection dialogs. Add the amount, pick the category, leave a note if needed, and save while the purchase is still fresh in your mind.

Spending Insights

See where your money is actually going.

Shinng turns your manual expense entries into clear spending breakdowns you can understand at a glance. Review spending by category, compare income and expenses, and spot patterns that are easy to miss when purchases stay scattered across memory, notes, or bank statements.

Budget Tracking

Know what is left before you spend.

Set a monthly budget and keep the remaining balance visible throughout the month. Shinng shows what you have spent, what is left, and the daily amount you can safely use, so every manual expense entry connects back to the bigger picture.

Calendar View

Review each day without digging through lists.

The calendar view gives every day its own spending snapshot. Check income, expenses, and totals across the month, then spot high-spend days, quiet days, and recurring money habits without opening a spreadsheet.
